# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, constructive feedback, accepting
responsibility, focusing on what is best for the community, and showing respect.
Unacceptable behavior includes sexualized language or attention, trolling,
insults, harassment, publishing private information, and other conduct that could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers clarify and enforce standards and may remove, edit, or reject
contributions or participation that is not aligned with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope and enforcement

This Code applies in community spaces and when an individual officially
represents the community. Report conduct privately through the maintainer contact
path in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly;
reporter privacy is respected.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers follow the Contributor Covenant 2.1 correction, warning, temporary
ban, and permanent ban impact guidelines, proportionate to the behavior and its
context.
